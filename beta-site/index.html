01The problem

Great conversations often go nowhere.

You meet valuable people, then details fade, follow-ups slip and opportunities go quiet. It rarely happens on purpose.

  1. You meet someone importantThe conversation is good and the moment is real.
  2. Life gets busyBy the evening, the details have started to fade.
  3. The follow-up gets delayed“I’ll do it tomorrow” becomes next week.
  4. A real opportunity goes quietNot because it wasn’t there, but because it wasn’t continued.

03Discretion

Discretion, built in.

For people whose name is on the line, how a follow-up feels matters as much as whether it arrives.

Your private note stays private.

What you write about someone is never shown to them and never quoted back.

Only facts it can quote.

Anything about their business comes from their own public website. If it can’t be quoted, it isn’t used.

No watching, no boasting.

A draft never mentions whether someone did or didn’t open their page.

You press send.

Every message is a draft for you to review. Nothing goes out in your name on its own.

These are how the prototype works today, and rules we intend to keep.

07Questions

Straight answers.

Does the person I meet need an app?

No. The card opens a page in their phone’s browser. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

What if their phone doesn’t support NFC?

Every card also carries a QR code that opens the same page, so any phone camera works.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. What you write about someone is never shown to them and never quoted back.

Where does the information about their company come from?

From their own public website, and only facts that can be quoted from it. If there’s nothing to quote, the page is written from what you told us.

Is it available now?

Not publicly. It’s a working prototype being tested with a small group. Founding members get it first, and we set it up with them.

What does it cost?

Founding members pay nothing. Pricing will be set after the founding group, and we’ll tell you before anything changes.
